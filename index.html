Chapter 01

Who I am

I am Muhammad Junaid Akbar, permanently curious. Before Computer Science, I studied pre medical subjects at intermediate level, so my mind still carries two different maps of the world.

For me, technology is not just a collection of tools. It is a way of thinking, solving problems, and turning ideas into something real. I have a strong foundation in Python, C and C++, and I learn new technologies when a problem demands them.

I do not define myself only by what I know. I consider myself a learner first. There is always more to understand, and I would rather admit that I do not know something and investigate it than pretend to have an answer.

Chapter 05

Why I build what I build

What interests me about AI is not only what machines can do. It is what happens to people as machines become more capable.

My goal is to build technology that helps people work better, faster, and more consistently without removing them from the process. AI should be an extension of human capability, not a replacement for human beings.

Chapter 06

What I love doing

I love creating things that are new and unique, and I love research on questions from every field.

Give me a computer, a quiet room, and a problem nobody has solved yet, and I will write code for something that never existed before. I want to become someone others can trust, honest, responsible, dependable, and useful to the people around me.

I don’t want to spend my life asking “what happens if we try this?”. I want to say “let’s build it and find out.”

I am a Computer Science student, a builder, a learner, and someone who wants technology to make human life better.
